Privacy Policy

Munajaa Publisher is a personal tool with a single user: its operator, Hamza Belhaja. It collects no personal data from anyone else.

Data the tool holds

  • OAuth access tokens for the operator's own TikTok, YouTube and Facebook accounts, stored on the operator's own machine and used only to publish his own videos.
  • The operator's own pre-recorded video files and their captions, until they are published.

No user data

The tool has no accounts, no sign-ups and no other users. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information about any TikTok user or any other person. It does not read other users' content, profiles, followers or messages.

Platform processing

Videos are transmitted only to the operator's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Facebook, where each platform's own privacy policy applies. No data is sent to any other third party.

Questions

For any privacy question, contact the operator at munajaa.dua@gmail.com.

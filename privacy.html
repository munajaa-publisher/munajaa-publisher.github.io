Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Munajaa Publisher is a personal publishing tool operated by Hamza Belhaja for his own Munajaa accounts on Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. It is not a public service and does not accept registrations from other users.

Information used

The tool uses the operator's account and Page identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, titles, captions and publishing settings. It keeps publishing records such as post identifiers, titles, times and results to operate and check the service. Authorization tokens allow the tool to perform actions covered by the operator's platform permissions.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to connect the operator's accounts, prepare and publish authorized content, check publication results and maintain publishing records. Account information and content are processed by the relevant social platforms and by service providers used to host and operate the tool. Their own privacy terms may also apply. Videos, captions and other material posted with a public audience setting may be viewed by other people according to the relevant platform's rules and availability.

The tool is not designed to collect other users' private messages, contacts or follower lists. It does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and requests

Authorization information, content and publishing records are retained for operating and maintaining the tool. The operator may disconnect platform access through the platform's account settings. For questions or requests to delete information held by the tool, contact munajaa.dua@gmail.com. Removing information held by the tool does not by itself remove copies already published on social platforms; those are managed separately under each platform's controls.

Munajaa Publisher

Personal publishing tool

Munajaa Publisher uploads the creator's own daily dua videos to his own TikTok, YouTube and Facebook accounts on a schedule, using the official platform APIs.

What it is

Munajaa Publisher is a personal publishing tool built and operated by the creator Hamza Belhaja for his own Munajaa channels. It uploads his own pre-recorded daily dua videos to his own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Facebook, on a schedule he sets. It is not a public service and it has no other users.

The tool runs on the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API. It signs the creator into his own TikTok account to confirm the account identity, then uploads his own videos to that same account, either as drafts (video.upload) or directly to his profile (video.publish). No other person's account or content is touched.

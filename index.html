Munajaa Publisher

A personal tool for preparing and publishing the creator's own Munajaa videos to his connected TikTok, YouTube and Facebook accounts through official platform APIs. Availability of each platform feature depends on its permissions, reviews and technical limits.

What it is

Munajaa Publisher is operated by Hamza Belhaja for his own channels. It is not a public publishing service and has no users other than its operator.

Platform connections

The tool uses platform authorization to connect the operator's own accounts. TikTok features use Login Kit and the Content Posting API, including draft uploads and direct publishing where approved. A listed feature does not mean approval has been granted or that every published item is visible to every viewer.
